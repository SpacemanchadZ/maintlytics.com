The app

Maintlytics runs on a Mac in your plant. Your work orders, schedules, plans and analyses are stored on that Mac. iPhones and iPads sync through your own iCloud account. Maintlytics does not receive, store or see your plant's data.

If you turn on optional AI drafting, the text needed for that draft is sent to the AI model you choose, under that provider's terms.

This website

When you request a demo, we use what you send us only to reply and arrange the demo. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

The site loads its font from Google Fonts. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies; your light/dark choice is remembered in your own browser.
