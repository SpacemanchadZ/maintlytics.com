How it works

No new system to feed.

Your techs keep closing work orders in the CMMS they already use. Maintlytics does the rest.

Save the pages you already run

The completed work-order report, the open list, the shift schedules, MTBF pages, the equipment and PM lists. Drop them in Downloads — or import a CSV.

They import on their own

The Mac hub picks them up, merges them with what it has, and keeps history. Nothing is retyped.

Every number is checked

116 self-checks run after each import. Counts are cross-checked, and Data health lists anything that could make a number wrong.

Everyone sees the same truth

The Mac, every iPhone and iPad, any browser on the plant network and the TV on the floor show the same numbers.
