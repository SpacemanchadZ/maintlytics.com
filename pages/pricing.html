Common questions about pricing

Is there a free trial?

Book a demo and we'll run your own week of exports through it first, so you see your plant's numbers before you decide.

What counts as a plant?

One site with its own CMMS data and its own Mac hub. Everyone at that site is included.

Do I need to buy a Mac?

The hub runs on any recent Mac that stays on in the plant — a Mac mini is plenty. Phones, tablets, browsers and TVs connect to it.
